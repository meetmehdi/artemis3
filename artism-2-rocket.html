ARTEMIS 3 MISSION

HUMAN LUNAR LANDING - PLANNED 2026

1

Historic Firsts

First crewed lunar landing since Apollo 17 in 1972. First woman and first person of color to walk on the Moon. First use of Starship HLS as the Human Landing System.

2

Mission Profile

Four astronauts will launch on SLS Block 1. Orion will dock with Starship HLS in NRHO (Near-Rectilinear Halo Orbit). Two crew will descend to lunar surface while two remain in orbit for approximately 6.5 days.

3

Lunar South Pole

Landing site in the Shackleton-de Gerlache region of the lunar south pole. Targeting permanently shadowed regions rich in water ice deposits, crucial for future sustainable lunar presence.

4

Surface Operations

Four planned Extravehicular Activities (EVAs). Scientific experiments including VIPER rover precursor studies. Technology demonstrations for ISRU (In-Situ Resource Utilization).

5

SLS Block 1 Configuration

Height: 322 ft | Thrust: 8.8M lbs | Payload to TLI: 59,500 lbs. Powered by 4 RS-25 engines (Space Shuttle heritage), 2 five-segment SRBs, and RL10 Stage 2 engine.

6

Program Goals

Establish sustainable lunar exploration by 2028. Demonstrate technologies for Mars missions. Build Gateway lunar space station. Create long-term human presence on and around the Moon.

7

International Collaboration

Contributions from ESA (European Service Module), CSA (robotics), JAXA (life support), and multiple international partners. Gateway modules from various nations.

8

Technological Advances

New Exploration Upper Stage (EUS) in development for Block 1B. Advanced boosters planned for Block 2. Enhanced avionics, improved thermal protection, and upgraded flight software.
